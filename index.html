Caring for your crochet

A few simple tips to keep your handmade piece looking lovely.

  • Spot clean

    Gently clean the crochet with a damp cloth and mild soap, then lay it flat to air dry.

  • Keep the wood dry

    Please don't soak the wooden ring or put it in the dishwasher, microwave or steriliser.

  • Check before use

    Look for loose stitches or wear before each use, and stop using it if anything looks damaged.

  • Always supervise

    Teethers and toys are best enjoyed with a grown-up close by.
